Engineered for cognitive endurance.

Essential tools designed to keep serious learners focused without algorithmic distractions.

Daily Study Planner

Schedule study blocks by subject, track daily hours against goals, and switch seamlessly between Gregorian and Jalali calendars.

Focus & Lock-Screen Timer

Reliable countdown and stopwatch running in the background, complete with a full-screen lock-screen alarm overlay with slide-to-stop.

Subject Analytics

Clear visual distributions of daily and weekly study durations per subject, tracking exact hours and minutes spent in deep work.

Bio-Routine Tracker

Log daily sleep schedules, track essential supplements or medications, and monitor meal plans to sustain cognitive clarity.

XP Progression System

Earn 100 XP per verified study hour to climb across 8 progressive rank tiers from Bronze to Legend without gamified distraction.

100% Offline & JSON Backup

Zero cloud accounts or analytics tracking. All database records live locally on your device with complete JSON export and import support.
